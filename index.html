    Rules script for Gordon to read

    Welcome to Coverage Bingo! Here's how it works. I'll read a short claim story. Your job is to figure out which coverage, form, or term handles it, then tap that square on your card. After each one, I'll give you the answer and a quick reason why, so even if you miss it, you'll learn it. When you get five in a row, type BINGO in the chat and unmute. Then read me your squares. Here's the catch: you have to explain each one to win. Some rounds come with a song clip that hints at the answer. Listen closely. Winner gets [prize]. Ready? Let's play.
